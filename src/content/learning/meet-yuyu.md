[MeetYuyu](https://meetyuyu.com/) lets people book good company whenever they need it: a verified, platonic companion for a wedding, a dinner, a concert, or a casual hangout, booked in under five minutes. The beta is open in Jakarta with no waitlist, and the site lists 200+ verified companions.

## My role

I wear several hats on MeetYuyu: I am the engineer who builds the product, the product manager who decides what ships, and the marketing lead who takes it to market. I also manage a team of four.

## How it works

Visitors browse a live companion feed showing each person's name, city, hourly rate, and interests, then book directly. Companions only appear once the MeetYuyu team has reviewed them.

## Occasions

The product is organized around real situations:

- Weddings and formal events
- Dinners and restaurants
- Concerts and theme parks
- Travel and a new city
- Movies and cafes
- Fitness and outdoor activities

## Trust and safety

Trust is the foundation, not an add-on. Four mechanisms carry it:

- **KTP identity verification.** Every companion submits their national ID and is manually reviewed before going live.
- **Two-way ratings.** Both sides review each outing, and anyone who behaves badly loses access.
- **Clear boundaries.** The platform is platonic only, and any request outside that is an immediate ban.
- **In-app chat and booking trail.** All communication stays inside the app, leaving a record of every agreement.

These are the same ideas I care about in compliance work: verified identity, accountability on both sides, and a traceable record.
