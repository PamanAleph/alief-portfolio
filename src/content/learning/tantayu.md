[Tantayu](https://tantayu.com/) is a transport management system for teams that move goods by truck. Orders, loads, trips, drivers, proof of delivery, and billing quantities live in one place instead of being scattered across chats and spreadsheets. It is in beta, and we are looking for our first clients.

## My role

I am a co-founder of Tantayu, and I also act as its product manager and strategic planner. I decide what the product does and in what order it ships, and I plan how it reaches its first customers.

## Why teams pick it

- **Know when a delivery arrives, and why it might be late.** Arrival comes as a range with a status and the reasons, not a single guess.
- **One flow from order to billing.** Nothing is retyped between the order, the trip, the proof of delivery, and the billing export.
- **Office and drivers share one app.** Each person sees only what their permissions allow, and drivers install it on their phone with no app store.
- **Built for real roads.** It keeps working without signal, and it learns from your own trips.
- **Audit-ready by default.** Every change is attributed, risky actions need a written reason, and customers only ever see their own order.

## Arrival estimates that explain themselves

Most tools give one exact time and are wrong. Tantayu gives a **range**, a **risk status** (On track, Tight, Late, or No update), and the **reasons**, such as "loading here usually takes 8 minutes" or "the truck left 20 minutes later than planned".

- It learns the usual travel and unloading time at each place from your drivers' own Depart and Arrive taps, including Ramadan and Lebaran patterns.
- Truck road times are used only after they prove more accurate than straight-line distance on your own history.
- With too little data it says "no estimate yet" instead of inventing a number.
- When a trip turns Tight or Late, the dispatcher gets a ready-to-copy message for the customer, a better stop order, and a latest-departure time. Tantayu never sends or stores the message.
- A daily planner proposes which loads go on which truck, keeping promised delivery times ahead of shortest distance. Nothing is created until the dispatcher confirms.
- A live fleet map shows who is running, who is late, and who has not reported in.

## Orders and trips

- Orders with several pickup and drop-off points. If one is only partly delivered, the remainder stays on record and can be rescheduled.
- One order can be split across many loads, and one trip can carry loads from many orders, with shared drop-offs.
- A trip wizard checks weight and volume against vehicle capacity. Going over the limit needs a recorded reason.
- Assignment is blocked when a required vehicle or driver document has expired, or when a vehicle is in service or a driver is unavailable.
- Orders can be amended after the customer confirms, with the history kept, and closed partially without pretending they were fulfilled.
- A daily summary lists what needs action first: failed deliveries, late trips, partial deliveries, and missing proof of delivery.

## The driver app

- Today's trips with accept or reject, then a step-by-step stop flow: Depart, Arrive, proof, Finish. "Finish delivery" stays locked until proof is taken.
- Proof of delivery with photos, receiver name, optional signature, and the quantity received per line, including damaged or short items.
- Failed pickups and deliveries are recorded with a reason, a photo, and who holds the cargo, so nothing disappears.
- Installable on the phone, with an offline banner and saved actions that sync when the signal returns.
- Privacy by design: location is captured once when the driver taps Depart or Arrive, never continuously, and is deleted after 90 days.

## Customers and billing

- **One private tracking link per order.** No login, valid for 30 days, replaceable at any time. It shows status, delivered versus ordered per item, and history, but never prices, driver contact, or other orders on the same truck.
- **Billing from accepted quantities.** The export uses what the proof of delivery accepted, not what was planned. If a proof is corrected later, the affected export is flagged for reconciliation instead of silently changing.
- **Five built-in reports:** on-time delivery, vehicle activity, driver activity, document compliance, and billing summary.

## Fleet and compliance

- Vehicle and driver records with capacity, vehicle types, and CSV import.
- Document tracking for STNK, KIR, SIM, tax, and insurance with expiry dates and 30-day warnings.
- Service periods and driver availability that dispatch respects automatically.

## Teams and access

- Owner, Admin, Dispatcher, and Driver roles out of the box, plus custom roles and groups.
- Work across several branches and organizations with one sign-in, with order data kept per branch.
- Invite people by email, with optional two-step verification.
- Indonesian and English, dark mode, and Asia/Jakarta time by default.
